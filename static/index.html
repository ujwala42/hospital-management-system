Activity A2.1: Functional Requirements Specification

System Overview

The MS Hospital Management System (HMS) is a centralized, cloud-ready enterprise web application engineered to digitize hospital administration, clinical diagnostic tracking, bed inventory, patient records, appointment scheduling, and automated billing.

1. Patient Records Management (FR-1)

  • Centralized digital onboarding of Outpatients and Inpatients.
  • Storage of personal demographics, blood group, contact info, and emergency contacts.
  • Tracking real-time patient lifecycle status (Outpatient, Inpatient, Discharged).
  • Instant historical consultation and invoice record lookup.

2. Doctor & Medical Staff Operations (FR-2)

  • Specialization-based doctor profile maintenance and fee assignment.
  • Mapping doctors to designated clinical departments.
  • Real-time status management (Active, On Leave, Inactive).

3. Appointment Scheduling (FR-3)

  • Automated doctor slot booking with patient validation.
  • Status lifecycle management (Scheduled → Completed / Cancelled).
  • Prevention of double-booking and schedule conflict resolution.

4. Clinical EHR & Prescriptions (FR-4)

  • Logging diagnosis notes, symptoms, and treatment regimens per consultation.
  • Generating digital prescriptions specifying medicine name, dosage, frequency, and duration.

5. Billing & Revenue Realization (FR-5)

  • Itemized billing generation considering consultation fees, bed rates, and discounts.
  • Multiple payment gateway handling (Cash, Credit Card, Insurance, UPI).
  • Automated Net Amount calculation with real-time financial status tracking.

6. Inpatient Ward & Room Inventory (FR-6)

  • Tracking General Ward, Semi-Private, Private VIP, and ICU bed availability.
  • Real-time status updates (Available, Occupied, Maintenance).
  • Daily rate calculations for inpatient billing integration.
